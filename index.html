For customers

Common questions

Why do I see a $5 charge?

When you tap your card, the machine places a temporary $5 hold to unlock the door. It’s not a charge. Once you close the door, you’re charged only for what you took, and your bank releases the rest of the hold. Depending on your bank, the hold can show as “pending” for a few days before it drops off.

The door didn’t open, or it locked again.

After you tap, the door unlocks for 15 seconds. If you don’t open it in time, it locks again and nothing is charged. Just tap again to start over. Any hold from the first tap drops off on its own.

I opened the door but didn’t take anything.

No problem. If you close the door without taking anything, you’re not charged, and the hold drops off on its own.

I was charged for something I put back, or the wrong item.

Our cameras track every item taken out and put back, but now and then they can misread. Please don’t tap again or reopen the door to fix it. Just tap the button below, tell us what you took, and we’ll review it and correct any error.

When will I see my final charge?

It depends on your bank’s processing schedule, typically 1–3 days. Your final charge matches the items you took, at the posted price, which includes sales tax.
