Questions

Common questions

Is it really free to host a machine?

Yes. We provide the machine, the products and all service. You provide the space and a standard electrical outlet.

How much space does a machine need?

About the footprint of a household refrigerator, near an outlet. We'll confirm the best spot when we visit.

How often do you restock?

On a regular schedule based on how fast products sell at your location, typically every one to four weeks.

How do customers pay?

By card or phone tap. The machines are cashless, so there's no cash on site.

What areas do you serve?

Austin, San Antonio and the surrounding areas.
